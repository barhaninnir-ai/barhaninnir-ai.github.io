Color Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Summary: Color Rush does not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device information to deliver and measure those ads. In the EU, the UK and on iPhone, you choose whether ads may be personalized.

1. Who we are

Color Rush ("the App", "we", "us") is a mobile game for Android and iOS, published by Nir Bar Hanin, an individual developer. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at barhaninnir@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect, store or share any personal information on our own servers. The App has no registration, login or account.

Game data such as scores, levels and settings is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

4. Your choices about ads

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the App, a consent form from Google asks whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy → Ad Settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: the App asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

If you decline personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in the United States and Israel), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through the ad settings described in section 4 or directly with Google. You can also contact us at barhaninnir@gmail.com and we will help.

7. Data retention and security

We do not keep any personal data on our own systems. Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the App adds new features. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date at the top.
